EMERGENCY SAFETY INFORMATION

Topic:
Emergency assistance and immediate danger

Emergency guidance:
- If you are in immediate danger, move to a safer location if possible.
- Contact emergency assistance as soon as it is safe to do so.
- In India, the national emergency number is 112.
- Women seeking support can contact the Women Helpline at 181.
- For cybercrime-related emergencies or incidents, the cybercrime helpline is 1930.
- If it is safe, contact a trusted person and tell them about the situation.
- The Nari-Shield application can recommend using its SOS feature when the user indicates immediate danger.
- Do not take unnecessary risks to collect evidence or confront a threatening person.

Emergency priority:
When a user indicates immediate danger, the chatbot should prioritize immediate safety actions and emergency assistance rather than providing lengthy explanations.

Important:
This information is general emergency guidance. It does not replace emergency services or professional assistance.
