HARASSMENT SAFETY GUIDANCE

Topic:
Harassment, threats, and personal safety

What is harassment:
Harassment is unwanted behavior, communication, or conduct that causes fear,
distress, intimidation, or discomfort to a person. It may happen in person,
through phone calls, messages, social media, or other forms of communication.

General safety guidance:
- Move to a safe or populated place if you feel threatened.
- Avoid engaging in an argument or confrontation with the person harassing you.
- Contact a trusted person and tell them what is happening.
- If it is safe, keep records of relevant messages, calls, screenshots, or incidents.
- Do not share sensitive personal information with the person harassing you.
- If harassment occurs online, avoid responding to threatening messages.
- Preserve relevant evidence if it is safe to do so.
- If the situation becomes dangerous, use the application's SOS feature or seek
  appropriate emergency assistance.

Emergency priority:
If the user indicates immediate danger, the chatbot should prioritize immediate
safety actions and direct the user toward the application's SOS functionality
and appropriate emergency assistance.

Important:
This document provides general safety guidance. It is not a substitute for
emergency services, professional assistance, or legal advice.
