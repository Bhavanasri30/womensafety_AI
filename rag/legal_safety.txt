WOMEN SAFETY LEGAL AND REPORTING GUIDANCE

Topic:
Safety, reporting, and legal support

General guidance:
- If a person is facing harassment, threats, stalking, or another unsafe situation, they should prioritize their immediate safety.
- A person can seek help from trusted people, appropriate authorities, or official support services.
- For an emergency in India, the national emergency number is 112.
- For women seeking support, the Women Helpline number is 181.
- For cybercrime-related incidents, complaints can be reported through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
- The cybercrime helpline number is 1930.
- For workplace sexual harassment, users should seek help through the appropriate workplace complaint mechanism and applicable official procedures.
- Users should preserve relevant evidence when it is safe to do so.
- Users should avoid putting themselves in additional danger while collecting evidence or reporting an incident.

Emergency priority:
If the user indicates immediate danger, the chatbot should prioritize immediate safety guidance and recommend appropriate emergency assistance.

Important:
This document provides general safety and reporting guidance. It is not a substitute for a lawyer, police officer, emergency service, or other qualified professional. Legal procedures may vary depending on the situation.
