STALKING SAFETY GUIDANCE

Topic:
Stalking and personal safety

General safety guidance:
- If you believe someone is following you, move toward a busy, well-lit, or populated public place.
- Avoid confronting or engaging with the person who may be following you.
- Contact someone you trust and tell them what is happening.
- If it is safe to do so, share your location with a trusted person.
- Keep your phone accessible so that you can contact help if the situation becomes dangerous.
- If you are in immediate danger, use the application's SOS feature or seek appropriate emergency assistance.
- If possible and safe, record relevant details about the incident, such as the time, location, and description of the person or vehicle.
- Do not put yourself in additional danger just to collect evidence.

Emergency priority:
If the user indicates that they are in immediate danger, the chatbot should prioritize immediate safety guidance and direct the user toward the application's SOS functionality and appropriate emergency assistance.

Important:
This document provides general safety guidance. It is not a substitute for emergency services, professional assistance, or legal advice.
